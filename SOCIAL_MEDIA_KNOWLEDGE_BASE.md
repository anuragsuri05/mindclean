# Global Social Media Knowledge Base

> **Last updated:** 18 October 2026
> **Purpose:** One reference for every major social platform worldwide: who owns it, how many people use it, who those users are, how it works, how it makes money, how creators and brands use it, and what risks it faces.
> **Data honesty rule:** Figures marked **[Official]** come from company filings or earnings releases. Figures marked **[Est.]** come from third-party trackers (DataReportal, Similarweb, Statista, marketing blogs) and can be 10–25% off. Many platforms (X, LinkedIn, Telegram, Meta's per-app MAU) no longer publish audited user counts.

## 1. Global Snapshot

| Metric | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Social media user identities | **~5.66B (Oct 2025) → ~5.79B (Apr 2026)** [Est.] | DataReportal / Meltwater / We Are Social. "Identities" ≠ unique humans (duplicates, business accounts). |
| Share of world population | **~69.9%** [Est.] | "Supermajority" status per Digital 2026. |
| Annual growth | ~294M new identities in the year to Apr 2026 (+5.4%) [Est.] | |
| Average daily time on social | **~2h 21m** per user [Est.] | Highest in Kenya, South Africa, Brazil, Philippines (3h+); lowest in Japan (<1h). |
| Avg. platforms used per person per month | ~6.8 [Est.] | |
| Meta "Family Daily Active People" | **3.60B** (June 2026, +3% YoY) [Official] | Unique people using ≥1 Meta app daily. |
| Main reasons people use social | Keep in touch with friends/family, fill spare time, read news, find content, see what's trending, find products. | GWI survey data |
| Mobile share of social time | >90% | Almost all growth is mobile-first. |

**Big picture:**
- **Meta** (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, Threads) reaches about 3.6B people every day. That makes it the largest social company by far.
- **Google/YouTube** is the biggest video platform. It also earns the most from ads of any platform outside Meta.
- **ByteDance** (TikTok + Douyin) leads short-form video and sets its format for the rest of the industry.
- **Tencent** (WeChat/Weixin, QQ) dominates China. WeChat is the model of a "super-app".
- Growth has largely saturated in North America and Europe. New users come mostly from **India, Africa, Southeast Asia and Latin America**.

## 3. Meta Family

**Meta Platforms, Inc.** (NASDAQ: META)
- **HQ:** Menlo Park, California. **CEO:** Mark Zuckerberg.
- **Combined reach:** 3.60B daily active people (June 2026) [Official].
- **Business:** About 97–98% of revenue comes from advertising. Reality Labs (Quest VR, Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses) loses tens of billions of dollars a year.
- **2025–2026 strategy:** AI is central. Meta AI assistant runs across all apps. AI-driven ranking and recommendations (more "unconnected" content from accounts you don't follow). Advantage+ automated ads. Large spending on AI data centers.
- **User reporting:** Meta stopped publishing per-app MAU after Q4 2023. Per-app milestones now only come from the CEO on earnings calls.
- **Disruptions:** Q1 2026 DAP dipped because of internet shutdowns in Iran and restricted WhatsApp access in Russia.

## 4. YouTube (Google)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| Owner | Alphabet Inc. **CEO:** Neal Mohan (since 2023) |

| Content formats | Long-form video (no practical max length for verified accounts), Shorts (≤3 min vertical), Live, Premieres, Podcasts, Posts |

## 5. TikTok & Douyin (ByteDance)

**ByteDance Ltd.**: founded 2012 by Zhang Yiming. HQ Beijing (legally domiciled in the Cayman Islands). Valued at roughly $300B+. Products: Douyin, TikTok, Toutiao, CapCut, Lemon8, Doubao (AI chatbot).

### 5.1 TikTok
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Launched | Sept 2017 internationally. Merged with Musical.ly in Aug 2018. |
| Users | ~1.7–2.2B MAU [Est.] |
| Largest markets | USA (200M+ claimed incl. businesses' reach), Indonesia (~150M+), Brazil, Mexico, Pakistan, Philippines, Vietnam |
| Banned in | **India** (since June 2020), Afghanistan, Nepal (lifted 2024), plus bans on government devices in the US, EU, Canada, UK and others |
| US situation (2026) | The 2024 "Protecting Americans from Foreign Adversary Controlled Applications Act" forced a divest-or-ban. TikTok went dark briefly on 19 Jan 2025, then executive orders delayed enforcement. **On 22 Jan 2026 the US business moved to "TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC".** ByteDance keeps 19.9%. Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX hold 15% each. New US and allied investors hold about 50% in total. CEO: Adam Presser. The JV retrains a US copy of the algorithm on US data under Oracle's oversight. A lawsuit (Mar 2026) argues the deal still breaks the 2024 law; it is pending. |
| Demographics | Core 16–34. Fastest growth now in 35+. Slightly female-leaning in the US. |
| Core features | For You Page (FYP), Following, LIVE, Duets, Stitches, Sounds, TikTok Shop, Photo Mode, longer videos (up to 60 min uploads), Search, TikTok Notes, Footnotes |
| Algorithm | Interest-graph, not social-graph: you don't need followers to go viral. Each video goes first to a small test group; if completion rate, rewatches, shares, comments and saves are strong, it expands to larger pools. Main signals: **watch time / completion**, replays, shares, follows-from-video. Text in captions and on-screen also matters for search. |
| Commerce | **TikTok Shop** (in US, UK, SEA, Mexico, parts of EU, Japan from 2025): live shopping, affiliate creators, Fulfilled by TikTok. US GMV is growing fast. |
| Revenue | Ads (TopView, In-Feed, Spark Ads, Branded Effects), TikTok Shop commissions, LIVE gifts (coins) |
| Creator monetization | Creator Rewards Program (videos >1 min; 10K followers, 100K views/30 days), LIVE Gifts, Subscriptions, TikTok Shop affiliate, Creator Marketplace / TikTok One for brand deals |
| Strengths | Unmatched discovery and virality. Shapes culture and music charts. Search engine for Gen Z. |
| Weaknesses | Regulatory and geopolitical risk. Brand-safety debates. Youth well-being scrutiny (EU DSA proceedings). |
| Best use cases | Brand awareness, trend marketing, product launches, social commerce, music promotion |

### 5.2 Douyin (China)
- The Chinese twin of TikTok, run separately. About 750–800M DAU/MAU [Est.].
- Much more commerce-heavy: e-commerce GMV is several trillion RMB, live-stream selling, local services (food delivery, travel).
- Strict real-name rules, censorship and youth mode (40 min/day for under-14s).

### 5.3 CapCut & Lemon8
- **CapCut:** the leading mobile video editor, with hundreds of millions of MAU. Templates and AI effects shape TikTok trends.
- **Lemon8:** a lifestyle and photo app in the style of Instagram and Pinterest. Pushed in the US and Japan. Moderate traction.

## 12. How Feeds Rank Content

Common ranking signals across modern feeds:
1. **Predicted engagement:** the probability you'll like, comment, share, save or watch.
2. **Watch time / dwell time:** the strongest signal for video. Completion rate matters most for short-form.
3. **Shares and sends:** sharing by DM counts heavily on Instagram and TikTok.
4. **Relationship:** past interaction with the creator (weighted more on Facebook and Snapchat).
5. **Recency:** strongest on X, Threads and LinkedIn.
6. **Originality:** reposts and watermarked videos are penalized on Instagram, YouTube and Facebook (2024–25 policies).
7. **Negative signals:** "not interested", hides, quick skips, reports.
8. **Integrity:** borderline content, misinformation and engagement-bait are demoted.
9. **Paid status:** Premium/verified accounts get a boost on X.

Platform transparency resources:
- X open-sourced part of its algorithm (2023).
- Meta publishes "system cards" for feed ranking.
- TikTok has Transparency Centers.
- The EU DSA requires a non-personalized feed option for very large platforms.

## 15. Regulation, Safety & Legal Landscape

### 15.1 Key laws
| Region | Law | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **EU** | **Digital Services Act (DSA)** (2024) | Very Large Online Platforms (VLOPs) must do risk assessments, give researchers data access, offer non-personalized feeds, publish ad repositories, and protect minors. Fines up to 6% of global revenue. **X fined €120M (Dec 2025).** Investigations of Meta, TikTok and AliExpress. |
| EU | **Digital Markets Act (DMA)** | "Gatekeepers" (Meta, Alphabet, ByteDance, Apple, Amazon, Microsoft, Booking): interoperability, data-combination limits. Meta's "pay or consent" model was fined €200M (2025). |
| EU | **GDPR** | Meta fined a record €1.2B (2023) over data transfers. TikTok fined €530M (2025) over transfers to China. |
| EU | AI Act (phasing in 2025–27) | Labeling of deepfakes and AI content |
| **UK** | **Online Safety Act** | Age checks for adult/harmful content (enforced from July 2025). Ofcom codes of practice. |

### 15.2 Ongoing safety issues
- Teen mental health. US Surgeon General called for warning labels (2024). Responses: Instagram Teen Accounts, TikTok screen-time limits.
- Misinformation and election integrity. In Jan 2025 Meta ended US third-party fact-checking and switched to Community Notes.
- AI-generated content and deepfakes. Responses: C2PA labels, "AI info" tags.
- Scams and financial fraud, especially in ads and on messaging apps.
- Child sexual exploitation (CSAM), sextortion.
- Data privacy and surveillance.
- Platform addiction and "dark patterns".

## 16. Industry Trends 2025–2026

1. **AI everywhere:** AI assistants in apps (Meta AI, Grok, My AI). AI ranking. AI creative tools (Edits, CapCut, Veo in YouTube Shorts). Fully AI-generated content feeds (Meta "Vibes", OpenAI **Sora app** in 2025). Platforms license data to AI labs (Reddit, X/xAI).
2. **Short video is the default format.** YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels copied TikTok. Durations are now growing again: TikTok allows 60-min uploads and Reels allows 3 min.
3. **Social commerce:** TikTok Shop, Instagram/Facebook Shops, YouTube Shopping, Pinterest. Live shopping is mainstream in China and growing in SEA and the US.
4. **Private sharing:** DMs, group chats, WhatsApp Channels, Close Friends. "Dark social" sharing (private, untracked) now beats public posting.
5. **Search moves onto social:** Gen Z uses TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Reddit as search engines. Google shows Reddit and forums more.
6. **Fragmented text social:** X declines, Threads (500M+) rises, Bluesky has niche loyalty, the Fediverse grows through interoperability.
7. **Age verification and youth bans:** Australia's under-16 ban sets a template. Device and app-store level age checks are under debate (Utah/Texas app store laws).
8. **Subscriptions:** Meta Verified, X Premium, Snapchat+, Telegram Premium, Discord Nitro, YouTube Premium. Platforms reduce their reliance on ads.
9. **Geopolitics:** The TikTok US JV, Russia's blocks plus the state MAX messenger, splitting of the internet by region, data-localization laws.
10. **Creators work like small media companies.** Podcasts are now on video (YouTube is #1 for podcasts). Creators run diversified businesses (products, memberships).
11. **Connected TV:** YouTube is #1 on TVs. TikTok and Instagram are testing TV apps.
12. **Wearables and AR:** Ray-Ban Meta glasses (millions sold), Meta Ray-Ban Display (2025), Snap Specs (2026), Apple Vision.

## 17. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **MAU / DAU / WAU** | Monthly / daily / weekly active users. Each company defines "active" differently. |
| **DAP** | Daily Active People (Meta's cross-app unique count) |
| **DAUq / WAUq** | Reddit's daily/weekly active uniques (includes logged-out visitors) |
| **ARPU** | Average revenue per user |
| **Engagement rate** | (Likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ followers, or ÷ reach/impressions |
| **Reach vs. impressions** | Unique viewers vs. total views |
| **CPM / CPC / CPA** | Cost per 1,000 impressions / per click / per acquisition |
| **RPM** | Creator revenue per 1,000 views |
| **UGC** | User-generated content |
| **Social graph / interest graph** | Feeds built from who you follow vs. from what you engage with |
| **FYP** | TikTok's "For You" page |
| **E2E** | End-to-end encryption |
| **Super-app** | An app that bundles messaging, payments, services and mini-apps (WeChat, LINE, Kakao, Grab) |
| **Dark social** | Sharing through private channels (DMs, WhatsApp) that analytics can't track |
| **VLOP** | Very Large Online Platform (EU DSA: 45M+ EU users) |
| **Fediverse / ActivityPub / AT Protocol** | Decentralized social networking protocols |
| **Spark Ads / Thought Leader Ads** | Paid boosting of organic creator or employee posts (TikTok / LinkedIn) |
| **Community Notes** | Crowdsourced context labels (X, then Meta and TikTok "Footnotes") |
